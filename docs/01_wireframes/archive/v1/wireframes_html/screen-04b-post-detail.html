Why I stopped planning trips
AR
Aanya R.
2 Apr · 5 min read

Three years ago I had a color-coded spreadsheet for every trip. Flights in blue, hotels in green, restaurants in yellow. It took me weeks to plan a week.

Then I went to Spiti with no plan at all. A friend had mentioned a village. I bought a bus ticket.

The best things happened in the gaps I hadn't planned for.

Now I book the first night and the last night. Everything in between I figure out as I go. It's changed how I think about trips entirely...

Kaza, 2024. Nothing planned.

The thing no one tells you about unplanned travel is that the logistics are easier, not harder. When you're not trying to optimize...

Title BEFORE hero · reading-first
Posts put the title above the hero image, not overlaid on it. Text is the hero of a post, not the photo. Inter for all metadata (eyebrow, creator, date, read time).
Body uses Fraunces · DD-026 · reverses general rule
Post body text is 14px Fraunces with 1.65 line-height. This REVERSES the general app rule that Fraunces is for display + h1 + h2 only. The reason: long-form reading wants a serif. Every serious reading product (Substack, Medium, Craft) uses a serif for body. This rule applies ONLY to post detail pages; other content types keep Inter for body.
Pull quote · ink left border, italic
Ink left border (2px), 16px italic Fraunces, soft indent. Lightweight — doesn't dominate. Inline image captions use 9px italic soft-ink color ("Kaza, 2024. Nothing planned.").
No sticky booking bar · no price · DD-027
Posts have no booking CTA, no price, no KYC gate. Actions live in a minimal engagement bar at the bottom: likes, comments, save, share. Following the creator is the implicit monetization — the post is free, the relationship is the product. Stories-only creators never need KYC.
