Body

We left Mumbai at 5:14 a.m. — the city's worst-kept secret is that it empties at dawn, and the Mumbai-Pune highway becomes a quiet, crooked ribbon between hills. By the time we hit Mandwa Jetty the sun was the colour of soft turmeric.

You can drive the full distance to Alibaug, but I never recommend it. Take the boat. ₹150 for an hour-long crossing of the Arabian Sea, with the Gateway of India shrinking behind you and the Konkan coast tilting up out of the haze ahead. It re-orders something.
