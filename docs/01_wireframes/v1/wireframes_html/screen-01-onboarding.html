A creator-first home for travel, food, fitness, and seven more worlds to wander into.
1 · Three-CTA welcome
Primary: "Create an account" (coral). Secondary: "I already have an account" (ink outlined). Tertiary: "Browse as a guest" (text link, underlined). Following Airbnb's onboarding pattern and DD-003.
2 · Why guest browsing matters
Indian users burn data on sign-up friction. Guest browsing drops the funnel loss from 40% (hard wall) to 8% (Airbnb-measured). Guest can browse home feed, discover, detail pages, and creator mini-sites. Protected actions (book/save/follow) trigger the soft auth wall (Screen 04e).
3 · Copy voice
"Stories, trips, and the people behind them" signals multi-vertical from the first screen. Not "Plan your trip" (travel-only). Not "Social app for travelers" (too narrow). The subhead explicitly names the 8 verticals as "nine more worlds to wander into" — this is the on-ramp to the category picker.
Visual identity
Warm radial glows in the hero area are the only decorative flourish on the whole screen. No illustrated characters, no emojis, no gradients on buttons. Premium feel from restraint, not richness.

1 · All 8 verticals shown, always
Even with only Travel + Stories live at MVP, users see all 8. Waitlisted verticals get a "soon" pill + creator count. This sets expectations honestly and creates anticipation. DD-001, DD-002.
2 · Selection state · ink border + coral ring
Selected cards get a 1.5px ink border plus a 2px coral outer ring (not the card itself — the shadow offset). This is the one decorative coral touch in onboarding; it signals "I did this" per the save-state coral rule.
3 · Waitlist copy is specific
"8 on waitlist" (not "soon" alone) because a number makes the promise feel real and trackable. When Food ships, everyone on the waitlist gets a notification — the count becomes real audience.
Progress bar · 5 segments not 4
Onboarding steps: phone → location → categories → creators → done. The addition of the mandatory location step (DD-009) turns v1.1's 4-step flow into 5. Progress bar is 3/5 here because user has completed phone, location, and is now on categories.
C · Milestone · final step
Welcome in, Riya.
Your feed is ready. Travel and Stories creators from Pune and nearby are lined up.
1 · Restrained celebration
No confetti, no Lottie fireworks, no XP popups. A single coral-outlined star mark with a subtle glow. Premium feel comes from restraint — the celebration is in the words ("Welcome in, Riya"), not the animation.
2 · Personalized subhead
"Your feed is ready. Travel and Stories creators from Pune and nearby are lined up." References the user's picks AND their location — the app acknowledges both. This is the "the app knows me" moment.
3 · Auto-dismiss option
Per DD-004, this screen can auto-dismiss after 2 seconds OR wait for the user to tap the CTA. Product decision: make it tap-required for the first session (users want to read the welcome), auto-dismiss for re-onboarding flows after settings changes.
The star icon
One of exactly five places coral is permitted semantically — this is "something the user has accomplished." The same coral rule as save-bookmark-active and overnight-itinerary-pin.
