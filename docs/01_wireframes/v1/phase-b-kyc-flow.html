The "Why we ask" pattern
When the creator taps the ⓘ link, an inline explainer expands above the field (not a modal — modals interrupt). It stays open until tapped again. Three sentences, three reciprocity statements: what it's for, what we never do, how we store it. Coral left border ties it to the trust accent. Validated state on PAN number shows green border + checkmark when format matches `[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}`. Uploaded state replaces the dashed dropzone with a green-bordered card showing thumbnail + filename + size. CTA enables only when all three are valid.

IFSC autofill is the magic moment
The moment after the creator types a valid IFSC code, the bank name + branch + city auto-populates in a green confirmation row. This single interaction is the highest-trust moment in the whole flow — it shows the platform knows what it's doing. Re-entered account number prevents typos in the most expensive field (a wrong account number = lost payout). Account holder name has explicit "must match PAN" hint to prevent the most common reason for KYC rejection in India: bank account in initials, PAN in full name.

Review with inline edit · no start-over
Each row shows a green check + label + masked value + Edit link. Inline edit is critical — tapping Edit jumps the creator back to that specific step with their data preserved, lets them fix one field, then returns them to Review. The declaration text is plain language ("documents belong to me") with one legal phrase ("punishable under Indian law") to make the seriousness clear without bureaucratic tone. Pre-checked checkbox is a deliberate choice — most users would check it anyway and pre-checking reduces friction. Two CTAs: submit now (primary), save and finish later (secondary text).

Rejection done with empathy
The rejection state is the most dangerous moment in KYC — most platforms drop creators here because they make it feel like failure. We deliberately don't say "rejected" in the headline. "We need a couple of fixes" + the reassurance "most issues take less than 2 minutes" frames it as a small task, not a verdict. Each issue has: specific field name, plain-language reason, individual Fix link that jumps to that exact field with the data preserved. The 12-hour re-review SLA is intentionally faster than the original 24-48 hours — rewarding the creator for fixing things quickly. WhatsApp escalation link at the bottom for creators who can't figure out the fix on their own.
Phase B summary
11 screens covering the full KYC mobile flow from interrupt → intro → 5 verification steps → submission → status states (pending, approved, needs fixes). Option A inline interrupt is the entry mechanism — verification only triggers when a creator tries to publish paid content for the first time. Selfie verification included as a step 4 with face oval guide. "Why we ask" expandable explainer pattern on every input gives creators control over their understanding. Manual review for MVP (24-48 hours), third-party API in V1 will reduce this to instant. Single KYC unlocks unlimited paid publishing — one-time effort, lifetime benefit. The flow is designed to feel like professional onboarding, not interrogation.
