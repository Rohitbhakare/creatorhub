Why we need this

RBI rules require KYC for creators who receive payouts via UPI / bank. GST regulations require us to collect PAN for TDS deduction (Section 194-O, 1%). Your documents are encrypted at rest, processed by our compliance partner, and never used for marketing or shared outside the verification pipeline.
