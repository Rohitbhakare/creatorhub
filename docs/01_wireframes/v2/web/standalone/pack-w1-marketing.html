Privacy Policy

CreatorHub Technologies Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what control you have over it — in plain English first, with the legal text available in our DPDPA disclosure (linked at the bottom).

1. Information we collect

When you sign up: name, phone, email. When you publish: content, location data of saved spots, optional KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar). When you book: payment IDs (never card numbers), traveler ID for slot-based experiences.

We never read your Instagram or YouTube content. OAuth fetches your follower count only.
