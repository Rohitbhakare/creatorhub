Hidden waterfall I found near Mulshi
Took the back road from Tamhini and stopped where the GPS gave up. Walked 20 minutes through ferns until I heard the water.

The waterfall isn't on any tourist map — locals call it Devkund-jhari. Best time is right after the first rain, before tour groups discover it. Park at the small temple, ask the priest for directions; he speaks enough Hindi.

Bring grippy shoes. The last 100m is slick basalt and one slip means a cold swim.

Design system compliance

Layout — when to rail vs. grid

  • Horizontal rails on Home (default view) — Hot near you, Trips from {city}, This weekend, sub-cat rails. Cards sized so 2 are fully visible with a sliver of the 3rd peeking (signals scrollability). Scroll-snap aligned to card start.
  • 2-col grids on filtered/destination views — frame B (sub-cat chip selected) and frame F (intent destination "Day trips from Pune"). When the user has expressed intent, surface the full inventory.
  • Section sequence on default Home: Intent strip → Hot near you → Trips from {city} → This weekend → Sub-cat rails → Browse by interest → New voices.
  • Following mode is the only single-column layout (chronological feed, social-style).

Coral usage — exactly 8 contexts (DD-013)

  • Primary CTA — "Pick your city", "Continue" (one per screen)
  • Active save/bookmark — filled star on saved cards (frames A, B, C)
  • Location pin — top bar city pin in every frame
  • Active bottom tab — Home icon
  • Unread notification badge — dot on bell icon (frame A)
  • Selected chips, selected onboarding tiles, intent tiles, card pills — all use ink, not coral.

Tokens — no hardcoded colors

  • Background: surface #FAF7F4 · Chips/intent tiles: sunken #F2EEE8 · Borders: border #E5E0D7
  • Headings: ink #2C2823 · Secondary: muted #6B6660 · Tertiary: softInk #9C9689
  • Free badge uses semantic success #1D9E75

Typography

  • Display (Fraunces 700, 34px): page heroes, onboarding question
  • H2 (Fraunces 600, 22px): every section title
  • H4 (Inter 600, 16px): card title (Fraunces here for editorial feel — DD-aligned)
  • Body Small (Inter 400, 13px): meta, helper text
  • Label (Inter 600, 11px): card pill (UPPERCASE)

Other rules followed

  • No gradients anywhere — image placeholders use sunken with subtle border-pattern shimmer (matches skeleton style).
  • One primary CTA per screen (only "Pick your city" or "Continue" — never two coral buttons).
  • Bottom tab bar: 5 tabs, raised center FAB on Studio, labels always visible.
  • Empty state: icon + title + description + CTA — never a blank screen.
  • Card pills are ink filled, not vertical-color-coded (DD-013).
  • Price formatting: ₹4,200 with comma · FREE uppercase in success green.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44dp: chips, header icons, FAB all comply.
