Spiti in monsoon

We left Manali at 4am, the kind of cold that needs you to grip the wheel just to feel alive. The road from Rohtang to Gramphu is still half-river, half-asphalt this time of year — and you're not sure which is which until you're already through it.

By the time we hit Sissu, the light had turned the kind of pale gold that makes every photographer pull over.

The dhaba on the right just past the bridge does the best trout I've had on this stretch. Order it whole, with butter and lemon.

Why monochrome? Stop type is differentiated by icon shape, not colour. Only "Overnight" carries the coral accent — it's the one decision travelers care most about (where am I sleeping?). Reduces visual noise without losing scannability.

What this wireframe locks in (open for change):
  • HostCard placement: directly under the rating line on every screen — Airbnb pattern that anchors trust before the long scroll begins.
  • Sticky bottom CTA: only on experience + itinerary + event screens. Posts have no bottom bar — engagement bar serves that role (DD-027).
  • Reveal countdown: appears on the meeting-point card whenever meeting_points.exact_location is set. Copy: "Exact pickup point shared {N}h before start" — N pulled from exact_shared_hours_before.
  • Coral inventory across redesigned screens: location pin (header + meeting point + spot map), primary CTA (sticky bar + soft auth), Follow button outline, active bookmark, overnight pin, active bottom tab, verified-creator tick. No leakage into text, links, dividers, or background fills.
  • Reviews block uses ink-only bars (no green/red rating colours) to stay in palette. Average + count is the headline; breakdown bars are secondary.
  • Static map placeholder: city-tinted radial gradient + plotted dots from spot lat/lng. Real Google Maps swap-in is a one-screen change once the GCP key is procured.
  • Spot editor: 4 of 5 stop types use surface + hairline + ink chip styling. Only Overnight uses coral tint + coral border. The rationale message in the wireframe is illustrative — won't ship in the actual sheet.
